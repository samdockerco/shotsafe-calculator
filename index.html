Recommended drives

Both types are great; pick what suits your budget and workflow. SSDs are faster and pricier; modern HDDs are reliable, spacious and more affordable, and plenty of pros (us included) run HDDs happily.

Solid State (SSD)
Fastest transfers and culling, compact and rugged; a premium choice, ideal as your main drive.
Hard Drive (HDD)
Excellent value and huge capacity, a reliable workhorse, plenty fast for storage and backup.
